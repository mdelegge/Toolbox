# Tower of Zenopus - Empty Room Contents

This is a list of 50 random items that could be found in the dusty, forgotten storage rooms of the Tower of Zenopus.

## The List
1.  A stack of empty, splintered wooden crates, stenciled with the faded image of a squid.
2.  A barrel leaking stagnant, black water that smells of old brine.
3.  A heavy, rust-colored woolen blanket, crawling with moths.
4.  A cracked ceramic jug containing a sticky, sweet-smelling, and mildly poisonous alchemical residue.
5.  A bucket filled with rusted gears, cogs, and bent metal struts.
6.  An anatomical chart depicting the muscular system of an Ogre, with precise mechanical augmentations sketched over it in charcoal.
7.  A three-legged stool that wobbles precariously.
8.  A rack of empty glass potion vials, most of them shattered.
9.  A single, heavy leather boot, its sole caked in dried mud and something that looks like rust.
10. A coil of brittle, frayed rope that will snap under any significant weight.
11. **(Useful)** A small, forgotten leather pouch tucked behind a loose stone, containing 12 silver pieces and a strangely shaped goblin tooth.
12. A collection of preserved animal parts (lizard tongues, rat spleens, bat wings) in dusty jars, now completely dried out and useless as spell components.
13. A collapsed wooden shelving unit, its contents long since rotted away.
14. A set of rusted, iron manacles bolted to the wall.
15. A cracked scrying mirror that only reflects a distorted, blurry image of the viewer.
16. **(Interesting)** A child's wooden toy soldier, clutched in the hand of a human skeleton slumped in a corner.
17. A tangled mess of copper wire, partially stripped of its insulation.
18. A heavy, lead-lined box, approximately one cubic foot, that is completely empty. It feels unnaturally cold to the touch.
19. A set of delicate, but rusted, surgical-looking tools: scalpels, forceps, and a small bone saw.
20. A thick, leather-bound tome titled "Celestial Cartography," but all its pages have been fused together by moisture.
21. **(Useful)** A sturdy iron crowbar leaning against a wall.
22. A broken clockwork bird that twitches a single wing when touched.
23. Several large, stained canvas tarps, folded neatly on a shelf.
24. A chipped ceramic basin containing a dried, dark purple stain.
25. A wooden box filled with dozens of non-magical, cloudy quartz crystals of various sizes.
26. **(Interesting)** A single, unlabeled potion at the back of a shelf. It glows with a very faint, pulsating blue light. (Could be anything from a *Potion of Growth* to a simple laxative.)
27. A collection of pewter plates and goblets, all tarnished black.
28. A heavy, grease-stained leather apron hanging from a hook.
29. A small, locked iron chest. The lock is rusted shut and there is no key. (If opened, it contains only sand.)
30. A stack of scrolls that turn out to be Zenopus's meticulously detailed (and wildly inaccurate) horoscopes for local villagers from a decade ago.
31. A broken spyglass with a cracked main lens.
32. **(Useful)** 50 feet of silken rope in excellent condition, coiled neatly in a dry, sealed box.
33. A glass jar containing a preserved, two-headed cave rat floating in murky fluid.
34. A sack of petrified, moldy lumps that might have once been goblin rations.
35. An old, tarnished silver locket containing a faded portrait of an unknown human woman.
36. **(Interesting)** A set of rolled-up blueprints detailing a complex, automated potato-peeling device. They are signed with the initials "T.G."
37. A shattered hourglass, the sand spilled out in a pile on the floor.
38. A heavy iron flywheel with a broken axle, leaning against a pillar.
39. A broken lantern with its glass panes smashed.
40. A small cage with a rusted-open door, containing a tiny skeleton that looks vaguely hamster-like.
41. A collection of different-sized glass lenses, most of them scratched or chipped.
42. A half-burned logbook. The only legible entry reads: "Containment procedure v.9 failed. The Miasma corrodes brass fittings at an alarming rate. Must procure more reliable smuggler contacts for pure silver wiring."
43. **(Useful)** A small, high-quality steel file, perfect for sharpening blades or working metal.
44. A wooden flute that is warped and can no longer produce a clear note.
45. A taxidermied cave badger, its glass eyes missing.
46. A set of block and tackle, the ropes frayed but the pulleys still functional.
47. **(Interesting)** A single iron key with a grinning skull for a head. It doesn't seem to fit any door in this room.
48. A box of charcoal sticks for writing or sketching.
49. A metal bucket half-filled with a black, viscous sludge that smells faintly of tar and decay.
50. A heavy, non-magical book bound in reptile skin, titled "The Ecology of the Amedio Jungle," with several pages on venomous flora and fauna bookmarked.
